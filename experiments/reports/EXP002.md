## Task Formulation

Multiclass single-label classification of an (Object A, Object B) pair into exactly one of 4 relations. Competition: https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/astroclimb | Shared task: https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/WIESP/2026/shared_task | Dataset: https://huggingface.co/datasets/adsabs/AstroCLIMB.

image + caption (figure images and caption text, either side can be either)
